## 2. Methodology & Verification Setup

### 2.1 Alignment Verification
`tools/metrics.py` implements two distinct spatial alignment methodologies:
1. **SE(3) Umeyama Aligned ATE (`ate_rmse_aligned`)**:
   - Computes closed-form rigid transformation: $\min_{R \in SO(3), t \in \mathbb{R}^3} \sum \| p_{\text{ref}, i} - (R p_{\text{est}, i} + t) \|^2$ with scale $s \equiv 1.0$ using `evo.core.geometry.umeyama_alignment`.
   - Used as the primary whole-trajectory competition metric.
2. **Start-Anchored Alignment (`start_anchored_rmse`)**:
   - Transforms all poses using strictly the initial transformation at $t=0$: $T_{\text{align0}} = T_{\text{ref}}(0) T_{\text{est}}(0)^{-1}$.
   - Evaluates open-loop spatial consistency.
   - **Critical Note on Blind & Gap Metrics**: In `tools/metrics.py`, both `blind5_rmse` and `gap_2s_rmse` are computed from `pos_err_anchored` (the start-anchored trajectory). This correctly models real-world operational performance where future waypoints cannot be used to retroactively transform the vehicle's position.

### 2.2 Degradation Model Verification
`tools/degrade.py` matches `configs/degrade_default.json` across all injection mechanisms:
- **Mounting/Gravity Rotation**: Uniform SO(3) rotation with ~10° RMS tilt ($\mathcal{N}(0, 0.1)$ per axis).
- **Progressive Noise**: $\sigma(t) = \sigma_{\text{base}} \cdot (1 + 0.05 t)$.
- **Bias Drift**: Random walk with $\sigma_{\text{walk}} = \sigma_{\text{base}} \sqrt{0.001 \cdot dt}$.
- **Impulsive Spikes**: Poisson arrival rate $0.02\text{ s}^{-1}$ with $5\times \sigma_{\text{base}}$ amplitude.
- **Dropouts**: 4 windows (t=10s missing, t=25s NaN [2s competition gap], t=40s zeros, t=55s repeated).
- **Blind Cutoff**: Truncation at $T_{\text{total}} - 5.0\text{s}$.
